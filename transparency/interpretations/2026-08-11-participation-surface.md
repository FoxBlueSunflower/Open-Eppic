# The participation surface: visibility affords participation, and the creator threshold is Organize

**Date:** 2026-08-11

## Decision

Two linked invariants governing what a member may do with what they can see.
First: **there is no visible-but-inert Element state.** An Element is either
creator-only (pre-activation or draft) or visible, and every visible Element
is **at minimum Collectable**. This binds all future design — no feature may
show an Element publicly while affording neither Collect nor Derive. Second:
**the creator threshold falls at organizing**, not at collecting and not at
publishing. Collect (gather any visible Element into an unordered Collection)
is ungated and available to readers; Organize (imposing order or hierarchy)
and Derive are creator-gated; Publish-to-Work follows an organized Collection.
Alongside these, the visibility posture that Phase 1 RLS implements is
ratified: follower identity is never exposed to anyone including the followed,
only an aggregate count; Collections are publicly viewable while organize and
edit are gated; pre-activation Elements and draft Works are creator-only.

## Commitments implicated

- **3 (what you create, you steward)** and **5 (no one flourishes alone)** —
  this extends the 2026-07-22 participation-is-constitutive amendment. That
  entry settled that activation opens an Element to *derivation*; this settles
  that visibility also opens it to reader *collection*, an exposure the earlier
  amendment did not reach.
- **6 (speak truth — especially about ourselves)** — a member never has to
  wonder whether what they can see can be built on, and a creator is never
  surprised by an affordance attached to their activated work.
- **12 (everyone is taught; everyone teaches)** — the Collect → Organize →
  Derive ramp makes reading-as-proto-creation a structural path rather than a
  hard jump.
- **1 (every person bears God's image)** — the follower-identity decision:
  ambient watching is not a relationship the watched is entitled to inspect,
  and an exposed follower set is correlatable in a small community.
- **Part IV Hospitality** — a gradual on-ramp rather than a cliff at the
  Space boundary.

## Conflict rules applied

Rule 3 (system-truth unconstrained; individual-truth dignity-constrained) on
the follower-identity decision: aggregate counts publish the pattern while the
set of persons stays private, mirroring the moderation schema's refusal of
correlatable per-member tokens. Otherwise direct application; the Collect /
Organize / Derive tiering resolves what looked like a formation-gate placement
conflict by locating the threshold precisely rather than balancing two goods.

## Options considered

- **No visible-but-inert state; Collect ungated, Organize and Derive gated** —
  *complies with and reinforces Commitments 3, 5, 6, 12.* Consistent with
  Locked Decision 7 and the 2026-07-22 amendment, and it maps onto Locked
  Decision 11's flat-then-ordered sequencing exactly: the build order *is* the
  reader-to-creator progression, with the gate at the flat→ordered boundary.
  **Adopted.**
- **A visible-but-inert Element state (published, closed to all
  participation)** — *violates the 2026-07-22 amendment and strains Commitment
  6.* It is the read-only publishing state that entry foreclosed, reintroduced
  through a side door; a creator wanting it is served abundantly elsewhere.
  Rejected.
- **Gating the creator threshold at Collect** — *strains Commitment 12 and
  Part IV Hospitality.* It puts a formation wall in front of the reader's
  first act and removes the ramp entirely. Rejected.
- **Gating at Publish instead of Organize** — *strains Commitment 6.* The
  authorial act has already happened by then; a member would do the creator's
  work and meet the gate afterward. Rejected.
- **Exposing follower identity to the followed** — *strains Commitment 1*
  under conflict rule 3, and is inconsistent with the moderation schema's own
  reasoning about correlatable identifiers in a small community. Rejected;
  count only.

## Decided by

The founder, on the assessment recorded in the journeys working notes
(decisions D7, D8, and the ratified visibility set).

## Consequences accepted

- **The invariant binds future design permanently.** Any later feature wanting
  a look-but-don't-touch display of an Element is foreclosed, and reopening it
  means reopening this entry and the 2026-07-22 amendment together.
- **Locked Decision 6 is unaffected and the invariant is worded to respect
  it.** A derivative is visible and Collectable while remaining closed to
  further derivation, which is why derivability is computed rather than
  stored — a distinction the prototype already implements.
- **Collections are public.** A member's flat Collections are personal but
  publicly viewable; this is disclosed rather than assumed.
- **An open rights question is carried, not resolved.** A Work published from
  a Collection of *collected* Elements raises a selection-and-arrangement
  question against those Elements' retained owners, touching Locked Decisions
  6 and 13 and adjacent to the Phase 5 attorney gate. It arises only at
  Publish, which is creator-gated, so no reader meets it unaware — but it is
  unresolved and must not be treated as settled by this entry.
