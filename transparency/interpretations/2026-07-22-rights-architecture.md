# Rights Architecture: Peer-Grant Licensing, Four-Strata Forkability, 18+ Floor

**Date:** 2026-07-22

**Decision:** The shape of Open Eppic's rights architecture is settled, and
Locked Decision 13 moves from "undecided but immensely core" to decided in
architecture (final license *text* remains gated on the Phase 5 attorney
review). Four connected decisions, made together:

1. **Direct peer grant; the platform is a registrar, never a rights-holder.**
   Activating an Element constitutes a standing offer of the Open Eppic
   Derivation License to that Space's members; each derivation is acceptance,
   forming a licence directly between original creator and derivative creator.
   The platform records, timestamps, and version-stamps grants and holds no
   sublicensable interest in any member's work.
2. **A base grant plus additive permission layers.** Layer 0 (on-platform,
   non-commercial, non-exclusive, venue-restricted, irrevocable as to
   derivative Works already made, withdrawal prospective-only) is the MVP.
   Commercial/bounty permissions (Layer 1) and external, off-platform
   permissions (Layer 2) are additional permissions granted by the
   rights-holder and merely notarized by the platform. The base licence
   carries the hook for these from the outset so no later layer requires
   re-consent from existing creators. Which permissions a Space offers is set
   at Space level, bounded by a Covenant floor; external agreements are always
   person-to-person and bespoke; the permissions active in a Space must be
   clearly stated to any member bringing content into it.
3. **Forkability is four strata, not one.** Code forks automatically
   (AGPL-3.0-or-later, LD 17). Content does **not** auto-replicate: a fork
   begins empty and creators consciously port their originals, with the venue
   definition ensuring peer grants remain valid at a destination the creator
   chose. Identity does not fork — forks operate under a new name; the mark is
   defensively registered and bound by a published non-weaponization policy.
   Personal data does not fork — member self-serve export only.
4. **Launch age floor is 18+.**

The venue definition is objective and self-executing: any publicly accessible
instance of the published software that preserves lineage records, enforces
the licence terms, and provides member exit qualifies, with an additional
objective cessation/insolvency trigger. Nothing certifies legitimacy, so
nothing capturable exists to certify it.

**Commitments implicated:** 3 (what you create, you steward — and we will
defend it; the registrar model keeps rights at the edges and the §103(b)
retained-rights clause leads the licence text), 5 (no one flourishes alone —
the peer commons is consensual, entered at activation), 4 (no one is held
here — self-serve export, conscious porting, forks that inherit no lock),
6 (speak truth — disclosure of a Space's active permissions before a member
commits content; no overclaiming of literal perpetuity where 17 U.S.C. §203
sets a ceiling), 1 (dignity — personal data is not a transferable asset),
9 (equal process irrespective of size; pricing may never gate being-heard,
appeals, exit, or portability), and the Aim's refusal of platforms that
extract from creators. Part VI (the fork provision) governs stratum 3.

**Conflict rules applied:** Rule 4 (balance, don't eliminate) for the core
mechanism — irrevocability as to derivative Works already made, with
withdrawal prospective-only, is the grandfathering pattern the Covenant
already names for stewardship-versus-stewardship tension, and it coincides
with 17 U.S.C. §203(b)(1)'s statutory treatment of the same problem. Rule 1
(examine assumptions) resolved the apparent conflict between a creator's
control and forkability: a fork receives no content automatically, so nothing
of a creator's appears anywhere they did not choose.

**Options considered:**

- **Adopted: direct peer grant, platform as registrar — complies.** Rights are
  held at the edges; the platform holds only the record.
- **Hub-and-spoke (creators licence the platform, which sublicenses derivers)
  — violates Commitments 3 and 5.** It reserves asymmetric rights over
  members' work: the CLA pattern in content form, refused permanently on
  2026-07-16 for code and refused here for content on the same grounds.
  Rejected permanently, not deferred. It would also have concentrated every
  grant in the company's estate, making the members' rights an asset an
  insolvency or an acquirer could reach.
- **Exclusive grants over a creator's own material (the Kindle Worlds and DMs
  Guild pattern) — violates Commitments 3 and 4.** Capturing a contributor's
  own originated material, or barring them from their own work elsewhere, is
  precisely the extraction Open Eppic exists to refuse. The licence
  constrains the derivative Work as a combined whole and never the derivative
  creator's own contributions.
- **Venue defined as "the service operated by the company" — violates Part
  VI.** On steward failure every derivative Work would be stranded, making
  "because Open Eppic can be forked, Open Eppic can be trusted" false for
  everything derived.
- **Venue determined by a community vote or a certifying body — rejected as
  capturable.** Any institution empowered to name the legitimate successor
  becomes the thing worth capturing. The objective definition needs no
  referee.
- **No venue restriction — rejected.** It would contradict the platform-scope
  claim in Locked Decision 6 and remove the meaning of expansion through the
  Platform.
- **Automatic content replication to qualifying forks — rejected as straining
  Commitment 3.** A creator's material must never appear on an instance they
  did not choose. Forks start empty; the community ports consciously, which
  is also how legitimacy is actually decided.

**Decided by:** Founder, with advisory assessment.

**Consequences accepted:** Dual licensing and feature-based open core are
foreclosed — the former structurally, since it would require the contributor
licence agreement permanently refused in Locked Decision 17. Premium tiers may
therefore consist only of hosting, capacity, operations, support, facilitation,
and infrastructure-cost-driven metering; source is never withheld, the free
tier is never deliberately degraded, and being heard, appeals, equal process,
exit, and portability are never paid features. Derivative durability is
Space-scoped: because derivatives are Space-locked, the fork provision
preserves community continuity through ported originals rather than through
derivative Works, and this limitation is disclosed to derivative creators at
the moment of derivation. The 18+ floor narrows the audience deliberately and
is revisited only as its own decision. The platform binds itself to a mark it
may not weaponize. Final licence language remains non-final until IP-attorney
review at the Phase 5 launch gate; the architecture recorded here is what that
review is asked to render into enforceable text.
