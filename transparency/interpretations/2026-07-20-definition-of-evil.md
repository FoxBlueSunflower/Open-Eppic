# Covenant Amendment: "What Evil Is" Added to Part III

**Date:** 2026-07-20

**Decision:** The Covenant gains a "What Evil Is" section at the end of
Part III, defining the moral terms Commitment 2 depends on. It establishes:
(1) evil is act or omission, never identity — a person's identity is fully
known only by God, so evil is named plainly, stopped, and always leaves a
door back; (2) six essential shapes of evil done — desecration of the
person, consumption of the person (sexual), theft of stewardship, false
witness, exploitation of desire, and calling evil good; (3) withholding
good as evil's second form, drawn from the prophetic pattern — the stranger
unwelcomed, the weak undefended, the stranded unsupported, craft hoarded,
truth unspoken — applying first and hardest to the platform itself; (4) a
two-line content measure: celebration (the Work may not invite delight in
evil as good) and manner (explicit depiction of sex and explicit
violence/gore are outside the range regardless of moral posture, on the
scriptural restraint model); (5) one absolute closure: content sexualizing
children admits no depiction/celebration distinction, no creative range,
and no appeal to fiction.

**Commitments implicated:** 2 (the section is its moral measure — and the
manner line materially extends its architecture: depiction now has limits
of manner, not only of celebration), 1 (act-not-identity grounds the
dignity discipline in moderation language), 7 (restoration coherence
follows from evil-as-act), 9 and 12 (named as positive forms of withheld
good), 3 (the manner line's floor of respect toward others' works).

**Conflict rules applied:** None — direct application. The section supplies
definitions the conflict rules presuppose.

**Options considered:**

- **Adopted structure (shapes, not restated commandments) — complies.**
  High-level and clear enough to bedrock moderation; specifics deferred to
  Content Guidelines.
- **Celebration-only measure (no manner line) — rejected as insufficient.**
  It would platform explicit sexual and gore content whenever framed as
  non-celebratory, straining Commitment 1's respect for persons and the
  trust Element creators extend in sharing their worlds.
- **Placement in Part I after the Aim — rejected on structure.** The
  section applies the commitments and therefore follows them; it sits at
  the end of Part III.

**Decided by:** Founder, with advisory assessment.

**Consequences accepted:** The manner line narrows creative range relative
to a celebration-only standard; some works publishable elsewhere are not
publishable here, deliberately. Content Guidelines drafting is now
unblocked and must operationalize the shapes, the two lines, and the
absolute closure. Open questions expressly not decided here: the
Content-Guidelines specifics (including derivative-context questions) and
all interaction between moderation and perpetual licensing.
