# Contribution Ledger

The public, append-only record of accepted contributions to Open Eppic.
See [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md) for what is recorded and why.

Format: newest entries at the bottom. Each entry records a chosen public
name (GitHub handle by default), the contribution type and description, the
date, and a link to the relevant commit, PR, or record. **No contact
information is recorded here.** Entries are never rewritten; corrections are
appended.
